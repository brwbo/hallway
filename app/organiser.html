    Responsible by design

    • A person decidesEvery match is accepted or skipped by a named human.
    • No scoresNobody is ranked or rated, and there is no reject button.
    • Synthetic data onlyEvery person and company here is made up.
    • Minimal dataOnly what the conversation needs. Nothing is kept on a server.
